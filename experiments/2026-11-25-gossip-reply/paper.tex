\begin{abstract}
We implement and test an opportunistic reverse-dissemination candidate as
an inert patch to Lightpool. Periodic gossip requests one unmarked reply
from the receiver's pre-admission broadcast queue. Replies spend existing
finite retransmission counters. A predeclared nine-point baseline tuning
grid compares delivery, conditional latency and measured offered traffic.
All 120 development originals were attempted: 119 completed and one
interrupted producer finalization remains unavailable. The primary
30\% loss, exchange-off screen failed both frozen practical criteria;
no held-out attempt was admitted. This does not establish equivalence or
exclude smaller effects. The tables derive from retained originals,
including failures. The candidate remains an inert patch. This is a
bounded virtual screen, not a deployment or ordinary-network performance
claim.
\end{abstract}

\section{Mechanism and related work}
SWIM separates probing from membership dissemination and piggybacks updates
on ping/acknowledgment traffic~\cite{swim}. Lifeguard studies local processing
health and failure-detection tradeoffs in a memberlist-derived system~\cite{lifeguard}.
Lightpool's extracted event core and canonical binary wire format define a
separate baseline. This study changes periodic gossip only. It makes no
novelty claim or unrestricted push--pull rumor-spreading claim.

A request starts with an empty typed tag (one body byte), followed only by
membership records. Complete validation precedes any reply or admission.
The receiver sends at most one packet to the datagram source, using its
queue before admitting incoming records. Replies contain no marker. Empty
or non-fitting queues stay silent. Requests reserve a byte and batch slot;
a record that fits only without the marker stays queued for other paths.
There are no empty polls, acknowledgments, retries, request caches, timer
state, recipient-knowledge estimates or replenished retransmission budgets.
Duplicate requests can each elicit one reply. Once queues drain, neither
solicitation nor recovery is possible through this mechanism.

\section{Pinned methods and populations}
The protocol baseline is \texttt{f707a5e}; \texttt{base} stores its full hash.
Tooling ancestor \texttt{acce830} adds six exact 32-node, 10\,ms latency
cases and changes no baseline core, wire or simulator code. The frozen
\texttt{reply.patch} and \texttt{PATCHES.sha256} identify candidate code,
tests, trace-marker support and regenerated candidate snapshot counters.
Both arms use go1.26.3 linux/amd64 and identical measurement code.
Development selection uses Python 3.13.5 and exact rational arithmetic.

Staggered joins precede a 50\,s warm-up. Twenty distinct nodes generate
8-byte metadata tokens at 50,54,\ldots,126\,s; the horizon is 130\,s. Uniform
latency is 1\,ms in development, node service time is zero, and independent
0/10/30\% datagram loss applies throughout. Periodic stream exchange is
on/off; joins and probe fallback streams remain enabled. Full alive
membership at warm-up is required. Four-second authoritative acceptance
uses frozen 620 (32-node) or 2540 (128-node) update/receiver pairs. Later
suspicions cannot change eligibility. Failed generation, invalid warm-up,
incomplete observation and missed delivery are distinct outcomes.

Per replicate, $M$ is misses divided by eligible pairs; $T$ is conditional
nearest-rank pair p95 acceptance latency. Both conditional pair and
all-receiver distributions retain non-completion counts. Over the closed
$[50,130]$\,s window, $F$ is attempted framed datagram plus stream bytes;
$U$ adds 28 bytes per attempted datagram. $U$ is IPv4 UDP sensitivity,
not TCP wire cost. Lost sends, markers, replies, probes and streams count.
Sent costs and whole-run totals remain alongside offered costs.

The nine baselines are the Cartesian product of RetransmitMult
$\{4,5,6\}$ and GossipNodes $\{3,4,6\}$. The reply candidate keeps defaults
$(4,3)$. Development uses paired indices 2 and 3 in each of six
loss/exchange strata (120 attempts). Protocol, fault and schedule seed
namespaces are separate; matching fault-tape ordinals do not imply the
same realized losses after traffic changes. Receiver pairs are dependent;
replicate means are the comparison unit, and strata are never pooled.

\section{Frozen selection and stopping}
For each development stratum, $L$ has both costs at most the candidate's;
choose lowest mean $M$, then $T,U,F$, retransmission multiplier and fanout.
$H$ has both costs at least the candidate's; choose lowest $U,F,M,T$ and
the same knobs. Null $T$ cannot win. Missing/invalid replicates invalidate
the stratum; no survivor averages or new knob values are allowed. Write
comparator overlays before any held-out launch; carry them unchanged to
larger clusters and changed latency. Identical comparators collapse.

The 32-node 30\% loss, exchange-off screen must be bracketed, reduce $M$
against $L$ by both 25\% relatively and 0.001 absolutely, and reduce $T$
by 10\%. Candidate $M,T,F,U$ must also be no worse than $H$ and no
no-more-expensive measured baseline may dominate both $M,T$. A failed
positive criterion is not evidence of no effect. Mean-only dominance with
replicate ordering changes is inconclusive rather than equivalence.

The frozen held-out plan has at most 234 additional attempts: six
replicates 101--106 of all 32-node strata; six of 128-node 30\% loss
strata; three 128-node low-loss replicates 101--103; and three 32-node,
10\,ms latency replicates 201--203. Both 30\%, exchange-off primary
comparisons must pass practical thresholds and paired, whole-replicate
20,000-draw bootstrap checks (seed 20261125, two-sided 97.5\% percentile
intervals). Regression checks use paired descriptive 95\% intervals,
$+0.001$ absolute $M$, $+5\%$ $T$ and $+0.0001$ fixed-exposure non-alive
fraction guardrails, with no extra exclusions when $L$ has zero. Insufficient
baseline misses, failed observations, crossed held-out cost brackets or
uncertainty beyond guardrails prevent a positive claim. Exact rules and
all unlaunched identities are retained with the declaration.

Maximum retention is 8\,GiB and active work 24 hours. The driver runs
serially, checks disk space, retains every original and diagnostic, and
checks restoration before deleting only disposable raw copies. It stops
launching at resource limits without discarding an in-flight overrun.
No automatic retries or outcome-driven extensions are permitted. The
first preflight launched zero attempts; its diagnostic is retained.
Removing duplicate exports of unrelated historical payloads allowed the
second preflight without changing a candidate or inference rule. External SIGTERM later interrupted offline verification after originals
18 and 27 had completed and been packed. Distinct diagnostics and offline-only
recoveries are retained. A one-attempt continuation then interrupted producer
finalization for original 28. Its final summary, producer status, resources and
exit observation remain unavailable. Controlled idle commands reproduced the
Linux Codex 0.172.0 pipe-mode exit-143 failure; a supported PTY invocation
with stdin closed completed the identical control, all 28 original
restorations, and an independent candidate regeneration. Pinned Codex
source and an upstream reproduction identify the pipe helper's parent-death
signal path; the local creating-thread exit was not traced. The execution
record retains these controls and the explicit reopening of unlaunched
cells. No original recording was repeated. Original 28 remains invalid,
so its development stratum cannot contribute a comparator or inference.

\section{Limitations and reproduction}
The two development replicates are a screen, not a powered estimate of
rare misses. A stopped screen does not test the held-out hypotheses.
Uniform latency, independent datagram loss and zero service time omit
ordinary scheduling, heterogeneous routes, shared bottlenecks, encryption,
clock skew and TCP packet loss. Simulation uses the same core/codec as the
ordinary driver, but the ordinary UDP correctness/termination test supplies
no network performance evidence. Homogeneous candidate clusters understand
the new tag; deployment and wire-version policy are outside this study.
There is no claim against every possible retuning, historical memberlist,
or the original papers' experimental numbers.

From the record, run \texttt{python3 verify.py NEW\_DIRECTORY} to restore
committed originals and reproduce pinned offline summaries and deterministic
rows. Run \texttt{bash run.sh CELL NEW\_DIRECTORY} to regenerate a retained
cell independently. \texttt{campaign.py NEW\_DIRECTORY} executes the frozen
campaign with original failure retention and resource checks; it never
reuses a prior output directory. \texttt{results.py RECORD NEW\_DIRECTORY}
derives the JSON outcomes and these tables without simulation. The pinned
\texttt{render.sh TOOL\_CACHE} builds this PDF; \texttt{document-toolchain}
identifies its engine and TeX bundle. Machine-readable artifacts and original
traces, not this unreviewed prose, are authoritative. The operator owns
wording and approval before any outward release.
