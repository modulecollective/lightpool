# Queued gossip reply experiment

Work in progress. The tested candidate is retained as inert `reply.patch`;
no protocol changes are enabled in the library. `base` pins the protocol,
`tools-base` adds six fixed 32-node 10 ms cases, and `declaration.json` fixes
the campaign and decision rules before its first original recording.
`PATCHES.sha256` identifies the frozen candidate and ordinary-driver test.
The candidate-only snapshot changes were regenerated by
`refresh-snapshot.go.txt`; the baseline snapshot is unchanged.

`bash prepare.sh NEW_DIRECTORY` reconstructs and builds both exact producers
and reducers. `python3 campaign.py NEW_DIRECTORY` runs the serial development
campaign, verifies every packed original, and writes comparator selections
before conditionally launching the declared held-out plan. Existing output
directories are refused. The runner preserves incomplete evidence and stops
for storage, disk or active-time limits. Failed attempts are never retried.

No results or performance claims have been recorded yet. Analysis, committed
restoration checks and the unreviewed paper remain unfinished.
